Hello, my name is Paul Alanis. I'm a Creative Director and Filmmaker from Texas. I’ve spent my career shaping brands through design, motion, and content that connects with people on more than just a surface level.

I don’t believe creative work begins and ends with a 9-to-5. The best ideas usually show up when you’re experimenting and taking risks, trying something strange, or following a personal curiosity that doesn’t fit inside a brief. That’s why I spend my off-hours directing, shooting, and editing short films, branded content, and experimental visuals. Those passion projects sharpen my instincts and keep me from drifting into “safe” or predictable thinking.

Whether I’m leading design for a major brand campaign or running a one man crew as director, DP, and editor, I thrive on pushing ideas until they’re not just good, they’re memorable.

And because creative work should be fun too, let’s play a quick game. Here are two truths and a lie about me:

1. My cousin has written and performed with big names like Sting and many others.

2. I worked on a commercial with Aaron Ruell, aka Kip from Napoleon Dynamite. "Your mom goes to college"

3. As a kid, I competed in a local skateboard contest where Tony Hawk was one of the judges.

Want to know which one’s the lie? You’ll have to reach out.
